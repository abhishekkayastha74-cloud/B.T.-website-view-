Notary Public · Gujarat

Authorized Notary Public in Gandhinagar

As an appointed Notary, services include the administration of oaths, attestation of affidavits, verification of true copies, noting of documents, and witnessing the execution of commercial and legal instruments in compliance with the Notaries Act.

Notarial acts require the physical presence of the deponent or executant along with original identification documents, and remain subject to professional verification and statutory rules.

Related Work

Scope of notarial acts

Affidavits & Oaths

Administration of oath and notarization of affidavits for court, government, or official purposes.

True Copies & Attestation

Verification and attestation of document copies as true records of the originals.

Declarations

Drafting and notarization of statutory and legal declarations.

Execution of Documents

Witnessing and notarizing the execution of agreements, deeds, power of attorney, and business contracts.

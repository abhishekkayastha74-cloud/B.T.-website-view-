USEFUL INFORMATION

Common questions

Does contacting the office create an advocate-client relationship?

No. A professional relationship begins only after conflict checks, acceptance of the matter and confirmation of the scope of engagement.

How are professional fees decided?

Fees depend on the nature, urgency, forum, stage, record and scope of work. The applicable professional fee and out-of-pocket expenses are communicated after an initial assessment.

Can the duration or result of a case be predicted?

No result can be guaranteed. Timelines depend on the court or authority, service of process, evidence, interim proceedings, conduct of parties and other factors. A procedural assessment may be provided after the file is reviewed.

Can consultation be conducted in Gujarati?

Yes. Communication and consultation may be conducted in Gujarati, Hindi or English.

What is needed for a property title review?

Provide the title documents, prior conveyances, revenue or city-survey records, mutation entries, encumbrance information, sanctioned plan or permissions where relevant, tax records, possession material and details of any dispute or loan.

What if there is an urgent hearing or limitation deadline?

State the exact deadline, case number, court and present stage when contacting the office. Availability and the feasibility of accepting an urgent matter must be confirmed; sending a message alone does not stop limitation or ensure representation.

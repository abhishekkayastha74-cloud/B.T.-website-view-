JURISDICTION & REPRESENTATION

Courts & authorities

  • District and Sessions Court, Gandhinagar
  • Civil courts and criminal courts having territorial and subject-matter jurisdiction in Gandhinagar district
  • Family Court, Gandhinagar
  • District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission and appellate consumer forum, as applicable
  • Gujarat High Court
  • Mamlatdar and Agricultural Lands Tribunal, where applicable
  • Prant Officer or Deputy Collector and District Collector
  • Additional Secretary, Revenue Department, Government of Gujarat
  • Gujarat Revenue Tribunal (GRT) for tenancy matters
  • Gujarat Real Estate Regulatory Authority and other statutory forums, where applicable
  • Sub-Registrar, stamp and registration authorities for documentation-related work

Listing a court or authority does not imply that every matter can be filed there. Jurisdiction is determined from the statute, relief, subject matter, location, valuation and procedural stage.

Land & Revenue Law · Gujarat

Expert Representation in Gujarat Revenue Matters

Representation and advisory work may be undertaken in matters concerning land revenue records, mutation entries, RTS proceedings, agricultural land tenure, Ganot and tenancy disputes, NA (Non-Agricultural) permissions, and proceedings before the Mamlatdar, Prant Officer, Collector, SSRD, and the Gujarat Revenue Tribunal (GRT).

Acceptance of a matter remains subject to conflict checks, jurisdiction, availability, document review, professional fees and formal engagement.

Related Work

Scope of work

Mutation & RTS Proceedings

Appeals and revisions relating to property mutation and RTS disputes in revenue records.

Tenancy & Ganot

Proceedings under the Gujarat Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act.

NA Permission & Land Use

Advisory and documentation for obtaining Non-Agricultural (NA) permissions.

Gujarat Revenue Tribunal (GRT)

Appellate and revisional representation before the GRT and other higher revenue authorities like SSRD.

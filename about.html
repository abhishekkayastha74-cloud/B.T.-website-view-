Advocate & Notary · B.A., LL.B.

I am Bhupatdan T. Gadhavi, an Advocate and Notary practising in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. Enrolled with the Bar Council of Gujarat since 2004, my practice includes litigation, advisory work, land and property matters, banking and finance, consumer disputes, family proceedings and legal documentation.

Each matter begins with identifying the material facts, relevant documents, applicable limitation, jurisdiction and procedural stage. Settlement, mediation and properly documented arrangements are assessed where appropriate.

The practice serves individuals, families, businesses, developers, financial institutions and other organisations. Every engagement remains subject to professional obligations, conflict checks and an agreed scope of work.

LEGAL WORK

Legal practice.

The following descriptions identify the principal areas in which legal advice, representation or documentation may be undertaken. Acceptance of a matter depends on conflict checks, jurisdiction, document review, availability and formal engagement.

01

Civil and Commercial Litigation

Advice and representation in civil suits and commercial disputes, including declaration, injunction, possession, recovery, summary suits, specific performance, partition, succession, contractual disputes, partnership or business disputes, execution proceedings, appeals, revisions, mediation and settlement. The assessment considers limitation, jurisdiction, pleadings, documentary evidence, interim relief and enforceability.

02

Criminal Law and Proceedings

Advice and representation in criminal complaints, FIR-related proceedings, anticipatory and regular bail, remand, discharge, trial, revision and appeal, as applicable. Work may include defence, assistance to complainants or victims, document review, written submissions and procedural applications. Strategy is determined by the record, stage of investigation or trial and the governing criminal law.

03

Family and Matrimonial Law

Advice and representation in mutual-consent and contested divorce, maintenance, domestic violence proceedings, child custody and visitation, restitution, recovery or execution of maintenance orders, matrimonial complaints, family settlement and mediation. Sensitive information is handled with confidentiality and settlement is considered where it protects the lawful interests of the parties and children.

04

Consumer Protection

Consumer complaint, reply, evidence, written argument, execution and appeal work in disputes relating to insurance, banking, housing and development, defective goods and deficiency in services. The case is assessed for consumer status, territorial and pecuniary jurisdiction, limitation, documentary proof, relief and the correct opposite party.

05

Revenue and Land Matters

Proceedings concerning mutation entries, RTS disputes, certification or cancellation of entries, agricultural land, tenure conditions, tenancy, non-agricultural permission and other matters under applicable Gujarat revenue and land laws. Representation may include matters before the Mamlatdar and Agricultural Lands Tribunal, Deputy Collector, Collector, Additional Secretary (Revenue), and Gujarat Revenue Tribunal (GRT) for tenancy and other matters within its jurisdiction.

06

Property Title Due Diligence and Registration

Title search and legal opinion for immovable property, including review of title chain, revenue records, Village Forms, 7/12 and 8-A extracts, mutation entries, property card or city-survey record, encumbrance material, tenure, non-agricultural status, town-planning implications and litigation. Documentation may include agreements to sell, sale deeds, gift deeds, release deeds, partition documents, development agreements, leases, leave and licence agreements, mortgage documents, powers of attorney, wills and cancellation deeds, together with stamp-duty and registration support.

07

Banking Finance Recovery and Mortgage

Legal work for borrowers, lenders or institutions as permitted by professional obligations and conflict checks. Services may include title investigation, mortgage creation or vetting, loan and security documents, demand notices, settlement documentation, recovery proceedings, SARFAESI matters, arbitration and Negotiable Instruments Act cases. The exact scope depends on the mandate and forum.

08

Insurance and Motor Accident Claims

Advice and representation in insurance-policy disputes, claim repudiation, consumer complaints, insurance recovery and motor accident claim matters. Work may include review of policy terms, proposal and claim records, medical or accident documents, legal notices, written opinions, pleadings and evidence, subject to conflict checks.

09

Business Contracts and Advisory

Drafting and vetting of commercial contracts, memoranda of understanding, partnership or LLP arrangements, service and consultancy agreements, employment documentation, notices, settlement terms and project-related documents. The drafting process focuses on obligations, payment, default, termination, indemnity, dispute resolution, jurisdiction and enforceability.

10

Notarial Services

Notarial acts may be undertaken for affidavits, declarations, copies, execution and other documents where legally permissible. The original document, identity proof, authority and personal presence requirements are checked according to the applicable notarial framework. Notarisation does not replace compulsory registration where registration is required by law.
